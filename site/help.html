Ghostwriter help

A simple document editor you share with your AI assistant. You write, it writes, and you both see the same page.

What it does

Ghostwriter keeps a library of plain documents made of headings, paragraphs, lists and quotes. When you connect it to Claude, Claude can list, read and edit those documents for you, and it can open the editor right in the conversation. Its edits appear on the page as they happen, with old text erased and new text typed in, so you can see exactly what changed. You can keep typing too, and both sets of changes are merged.

Before you start

  • A PoLR Studio account. Ghostwriter is for PoLR Studio members, so you sign in with that account.
  • A Claude account. Connectors work in Claude on the web, desktop and mobile.

Connect it to Claude

  1. In Claude, open Customize > Connectors and find Ghostwriter in the directory.
  2. Click Connect. Ghostwriter asks you to sign in with your PoLR Studio account.
  3. Approve Claude's access. You only need to do this once.
  4. In a chat, turn on Ghostwriter from the + menu under Connectors, then ask for what you want.

You can also use Ghostwriter on its own in your browser at this address. Your documents are the same in both places.

Things to try

Open Ghostwriter and start a packing list for a weekend camping trip.
In my "Q3 plan" document, rewrite the Goals section so each goal is one short sentence.
Find every place in my meeting notes that mentions the budget and turn them into a bulleted list at the end.
Make the paragraph I've selected friendlier.
Which of my documents haven't I touched this month?

What Claude can do

  • Read: list your documents, read a whole document or one section, and search within it. These never change anything.
  • Edit: create a document, add or rewrite paragraphs, headings and list items, restructure a section, retitle a document and delete blocks. Claude asks before it makes a change unless you've told it otherwise.
  • Show: open the editor in the conversation, or in the side panel, so you can watch and type along.

Ghostwriter can't send, share, publish or buy anything. It works only on your own documents.

Comments

Select some text and click Comment (or press Ctrl+Alt+M, Cmd+Option+M on a Mac) to leave a note on it. Comments appear in the side panel, and the words they point to are highlighted. You can reply, edit your own comments, resolve a thread when it's done and reopen it later.

A comment can also be a request for your assistant:

  • Ask AI to revise asks Claude to rewrite the commented words using your comment as the brief. When it's done, the comment shows the change, and Revert puts the old words back.
  • Ask AI to answer asks Claude to reply in the thread without touching the document.

In Claude, the request is sent to your chat. Anywhere else, it waits in the comment until you ask your assistant to look at your comments. If the words a comment points to are deleted, the comment stays and shows the original words.

Undo and removed documents

In the editor, undo covers Claude's edits as well as yours. If you remove a document from the home screen, you can bring it back from the notice that appears. Removed documents are deleted for good after 30 days.

Troubleshooting

The editor in the chat is blank or stuck

Ask Claude to open the document again. Each time it opens, you get a new card, and only the newest one stays live.

Claude says it can't find a document

Ask it to list your documents first. Each person only sees their own library, so a document someone else made won't show up for you.

Claude can't connect, or keeps asking you to sign in

Go to Customize > Connectors, disconnect Ghostwriter, then connect it again. If signing in fails, check that you can sign in to your PoLR Studio account directly.

The editor looks out of date after an update

Disconnect and reconnect Ghostwriter so Claude picks up the newest version.

A comment says it's waiting for the assistant

Ghostwriter doesn't run an AI itself. Ask Claude to "handle my Ghostwriter comments" and it picks up anything waiting. To take the request back, click Cancel request on the comment.

A comment says the text was deleted

The words it pointed to are gone. The comment keeps a copy of them, so you can still read it, reply and resolve it.

Stop an app from using your documents

Open the menu in Ghostwriter, choose Connected apps, and click Disconnect next to the app. It loses access straight away.

Limits

  • Plain text only, so no bold, italics or images.
  • Up to 200 documents per person.
  • If you and Claude change the very same paragraph at the same moment, the change saved first is kept.
