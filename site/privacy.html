Privacy

Last updated November 23, 2026. Ghostwriter is made by PoLR Studio (polrstudio.com).

Ghostwriter is a document editor that you and an AI assistant, such as Claude, can work in together. This page explains what it keeps, why, who else is involved, and how long things stay around. The short version is that it keeps your documents and enough about your account to know they're yours. Nothing else.

What we collect

Your account

You sign in with your PoLR Studio account. When you do, Ghostwriter gets an account id, your name, your email address and whether that email has been verified. It uses these to know which documents are yours and to show you who you're signed in as.

Your documents

Ghostwriter stores the title and text of every document you create, along with the edits you and your assistant make to them. Your documents are private to your account. Other people who use Ghostwriter can't see, open or change them, and neither can their assistants.

Apps you connect

When you connect an assistant such as Claude, Ghostwriter records which app you approved and when it was last used, so the Connected apps menu can list it and you can disconnect it. The access tokens it gives that app are stored only in hashed form.

Server logs

Like most web services, the server keeps short operational logs: the time, the address a request came from, the page or endpoint, the response code and the app's name. These logs never include your document text or access tokens. We use them to keep the service running and to investigate abuse.

What we don't collect

  • Ghostwriter doesn't read your conversations with your assistant. It only receives the specific requests your assistant sends it, such as "read this document" or "replace this paragraph".
  • It doesn't read your assistant's memory, chat history or other files.
  • It has no ads, no tracking pixels and no analytics service.
  • It doesn't build a profile of you, and it doesn't sell or rent your data.

How we use it

Only to run Ghostwriter: to store your documents, show them to you, let the assistants you connect read and edit them, and keep the service secure. We don't use your documents to train AI models.

Who else is involved

  • Your AI assistant. When you ask an assistant to read or edit a document, the text it reads is sent to that assistant and handled under its own privacy policy. For Claude, that's Anthropic's privacy policy. You choose which assistants to connect, and you can disconnect them from the menu at any time.
  • Hosting and network. Ghostwriter runs on a server PoLR Studio operates, with traffic passing through Cloudflare, which protects and delivers the site.
  • Fonts. Pages load their typefaces from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google to fetch them. No document content goes to Google.
  • The law. We'd share information only if a valid legal request required it.

How long we keep it

  • Documents stay until you remove them. A removed document can be restored for 30 days, and after that it's deleted for good.
  • Connected apps stay until you disconnect them. An app stays signed in for up to 30 days, then you sign in again.
  • Server logs are kept for no more than 30 days.
  • Your account and everything in it can be deleted at any time. Just email us.

Your choices

You can read, change, copy and remove your documents whenever you like, and disconnect any app from the Connected apps menu. To get a copy of your data or delete your account, email us and we'll take care of it within 30 days.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Sign-in uses OAuth 2.0, and tokens are stored hashed. Each person's documents are kept separately, and every request is checked against the signed-in account. If you find a security problem, please email us.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll update the date at the top and say what changed here.
